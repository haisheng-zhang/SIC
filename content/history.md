How It All Started
The Innovation Journey Of Singapore Enterprises 
During Covid-19, a group of local food manufacturers under Singapore Food Manufacturers’ Association (SFMA) developed and launched an e-commerce platform, Singapore Food United (SFU), to facilitate buying and selling of food products between members through a digital platform. 

Inline with Budget 2023’s Enterprise Innovation Scheme, Singapore Innovation Centre (SIC) is setup with the vision to be the Go-To-Experts for enterprise excellence, lead in digital transformation roadmap, innovative creation for growth to foster cross border collaborations. SIC will also manage on-boarding more companies to leverage off the SFU platform and foster cross border collaborations across different industries. 

Singapore Innovation Centre (SIC) is supported by the Singapore Food Association Alliance (SFAA) spearheaded by SFMA and 7 TACs – Confectionery Dealers’ Association, Singapore Bakery & Confectionery Trade Association, Singapore Noodles Manufacturers’ Association, Meat Traders’ Association Singapore, Seafood Industries Association Singapore, Woodlands East Industrial & Commercial Association  and Bedok Industrial Association. The partnership will foster various stakeholders be it large, medium, small and micro in sizes together to work on projects that will benefit the industry.

SERVICES
(Source: SIC_Website(Content)R.docx — the old sitemap's four service pillars. Wording below is condensed for the site; facts and programme names are as stated in the source document.)

BUSINESS TRANSFORMATION
- Innovation & Productivity: working with enterprises to shift organisational mindset, sharpen communication, adopt tools for full visibility, and rethink productivity for a flexible workplace.
- Product Development: partnering from identifying a market need through quantifying the opportunity, validating the solution, building a roadmap, and iterating an MVP.
- Enterprise Sustainability / Energy Efficiency: guidance on using less energy to do the same work, lowering bills and moving toward net-zero.

INTERNATIONALISATION
- Digital Commerce Globalisation: SIC's programme with Coupang Co., Ltd (Korea), run under the Grow Digital initiative with IMDA and Enterprise Singapore, helping SFMA members engage Coupang's B2B and B2C platform.
- Market Readiness Assistance (MRA) Grant: Enterprise Singapore grant, up to 50% of eligible costs, capped at S$100,000 per company per new market — overseas market promotion (capped S$20,000), overseas business development (capped S$50,000), overseas market set-up (capped S$30,000).
- Business Matching: connecting enterprises with complementary business capabilities and interests, typically via structured sessions where participants supply their needs/offerings in advance.
- Intellectual Property & Franchising: protecting inventions and creative work; guidance on franchise licensing arrangements.
- Global Market Expansion: guidance for enterprises entering new overseas markets — strategy, localisation, business culture.
- Halal Certification & Supply Chain: certification guidance and supply-chain traceability from raw ingredients through to the consumer.
- Study Trip & Trade Mission: organised overseas visits — site tours, networking receptions, meetings with prospective clients/agents identified ahead of the trip.

SKILLS DEVELOPMENT
- Job Redesign & Career Conversion: SIC has been issued a Letter of Offer (LOO) by Workforce Singapore (WSG) as a programme partner for Career Conversion Programmes (CCP), initially for the Food Services industry — training and upskilling current employees and drawing in new entrants and mid-career switchers.
- Workshops & Masterclasses: e.g. "North Asia Opportunities: Future of O2O Commerce in Korea" for SMEs and professionals in Food, TCM (Consumables), Beauty and Wellness — three segments: O2O Commerce (business digitalisation and innovation), Marketing Research and Export Expansion, and Automating Processes.
- Training & Apprenticeship: named in the sitemap as programme areas; no further detail in the source document.
- SkillsFuture Enterprise Credit (SFEC): named as a Skills Development line item in "SIC_Brochure.pptx" — the national one-off credit for employers to defray workforce/enterprise transformation costs, including training. Not SIC's own programme; SIC's role is guidance on tapping it.

INNOVATION
- Open Innovation Challenge, Global Innovation Alliance, Innovation Lab, CTO-as-a-Service, National Innovation Award: named in the sitemap as programme areas; no further detail in the source document. Treated on the site as "in development" until a fuller description exists.
