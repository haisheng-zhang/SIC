Services

What we help enterprises do

Four areas of support, from day-to-day capability building to overseas expansion. Items marked In development are being scoped and not yet open for engagement — contact us to ask about timing.

01

Business Transformation

Shifting the organisational mindset, tools and processes an enterprise needs to grow.

Innovation & Productivity

Working with you to shift organisational mindset, sharpen communication, adopt tools for full visibility, and rethink what productivity means for a flexible workplace.

Product Development

Partnering across the full journey — identifying a market need, quantifying the opportunity, validating the solution, building a roadmap, and iterating an MVP with real users.

Enterprise Sustainability & Energy Efficiency

Guidance on using less energy to do the same work — lowering bills, improving competitiveness, and moving toward net-zero.

02

Internationalisation

Taking Singapore enterprises into new markets, and helping overseas platforms reach Singapore members.

Digital Commerce Globalisation

SIC's programme with Coupang, Korea's leading e-commerce platform, run under the Grow Digital initiative with IMDA and Enterprise Singapore — helping SFMA members engage Coupang's B2B and B2C channels.

Market Readiness Assistance (MRA) Grant

Support accessing Enterprise Singapore's MRA grant — up to 50% of eligible costs, capped at S$100,000 per company per new market: overseas market promotion (S$20k), business development (S$50k), and market set-up (S$30k).

Halal Certification & Supply Chain

Guidance toward Halal certification and traceability, from raw ingredients through manufacturing, packaging and distribution.

Business Matching

Connecting enterprises with complementary capabilities and interests — structured sessions where both sides come prepared with clear needs and offerings.

Study Trip & Trade Mission

Organised overseas visits — site tours, networking receptions, and introductions to prospective clients, agents and partners identified ahead of the trip.

IP & Franchising In development

Guidance on protecting inventions and creative work, and on structuring franchise licensing arrangements for expansion.

03

Skills Development

Building the digital and innovation capabilities enterprises need to transform.

Job Redesign & Career Conversion

SIC has been issued a Letter of Offer by Workforce Singapore (WSG) as a programme partner for Career Conversion Programmes in the food services industry — upskilling current staff and bringing in mid-career switchers.

Workshops & Masterclasses

Practical sessions such as North Asia Opportunities: Future of O2O Commerce in Korea, run across three segments — digitalisation and innovation, marketing research and export expansion, and process automation.

Training & Apprenticeship In development

Structured training and apprenticeship pathways, extending the workshop programme into ongoing capability building.

SkillsFuture Enterprise Credit

Guidance on tapping SFEC, the national one-off credit that helps eligible enterprises defray the cost of workforce and enterprise transformation, including training under the programmes above.

04

Innovation

Longer-horizon programmes for enterprises building genuinely new products and capabilities.

Open Innovation Challenge In development

A structured challenge format connecting enterprises with solvers on specific problems.

Global Innovation Alliance In development

Cross-border links to innovation ecosystems beyond Singapore.

Innovation Lab In development

A hands-on space for prototyping and testing new ideas.

CTO-as-a-Service In development

Fractional technical leadership for enterprises without an in-house CTO.
