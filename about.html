About SIC

How it all started

The innovation journey of Singapore enterprises.

During Covid-19, a group of local food manufacturers under the Singapore Food Manufacturers' Association (SFMA) developed and launched an e-commerce platform, Singapore Food United (SFU), to facilitate the buying and selling of food products between members through a digital platform.

In line with Budget 2023's Enterprise Innovation Scheme, Singapore Innovation Centre (SIC) is set up with the vision to be the go-to experts for enterprise excellence, lead in the digital transformation roadmap, and drive innovative creation for growth to foster cross-border collaborations. SIC will also manage the onboarding of more companies to leverage off the SFU platform and foster cross-border collaborations across different industries.

Singapore Innovation Centre is supported by the Singapore Food Association Alliance (SFAA), spearheaded by SFMA and 7 trade associations. The partnership will foster various stakeholders, be it large, medium, small or micro in size, together to work on projects that will benefit the industry.
