Platform

Singapore Food United

An e-commerce platform launched in 2020, endorsed by the Singapore Food Manufacturers' Association (SFMA) and patronised by Ms. Low Yen Ling — Minister of State, Ministry of Trade and Industry and Ministry of Culture, Community and Youth.

Developed and launched by a group of local food manufacturers under SFMA during Covid-19, to facilitate the buying and selling of food products between members through a digital platform. SIC manages the onboarding of more companies to leverage off the SFU platform.
